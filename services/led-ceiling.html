LED Ceiling Feature

A ceiling feature is millwork plus light. We build the cove or tray so the diodes stay hidden, the wash is even, and the driver can be reached later. New wiring is completed by a licensed electrician.

How we build it

  1. Define the effect

    Perimeter wash, tray over a dining table, or a linear slot. Direct glare vs. indirect bounce changes the profile.

  2. Measure the ceiling

    Flatness, existing lighting, HVAC, and where a driver can live without being buried.

  3. Specify the light

    Warm, neutral, or cool LED, dimming method, and aluminum channel with a diffuser so you do not see dots.

  4. Coordinate electrical

    Line-voltage, new dimmers, or a new circuit are scoped with a licensed electrician. We do not treat millwork drawings as electrical plans.

  5. Concept and estimate

    You review a visual and a written scope for millwork plus lighting coordination.

  6. Build the tray or slot

    Corners first, then straight runs, sealed so light does not leak at joints.

  7. Install and test

    Strips and drivers go in before final closure. We check color match, dimming, and shadows.

  8. Finish and walkthrough

    Paint or wood faces, diffuser on, and a simple explanation of how you dim the feature.
