Makeup Vanity

A makeup station only works if the seat, the surface, the mirror, and the light are planned together. We measure the wall, confirm outlets, and build millwork that fits the chair you actually use.

How we build it

  1. Consult and photograph the wall

    We confirm where you sit, natural light, door swings, and whether this is a bedroom desk vanity or a bath millwork piece.

  2. Field measurements

    Wall width at more than one height, depth, floor level, outlet locations, and the chair you will use. For bath vanities we also record drain and supply positions.

  3. Layout and storage plan

    Drawer banks beside a clear knee space. Shallow trays for cosmetics, deeper storage for tools, and a charging location if you want it hidden.

  4. Concept review

    You review a visual direction for wood, paint, stone or quartz, mirror, and lighting. This is a concept, not a construction document.

  5. Estimate and materials

    Written scope after the layout is agreed. Work does not start until you approve it.

  6. Fabricate and finish

    Carcass, drawers, and faces are built and finished so color and sheen match the room.

  7. Install, scribe, and light

    Level and shim, scribe to walls, protect the finish, set the top, then place the mirror. Side lighting is aimed for the seated face. New circuits are handled by a licensed electrician when required.

  8. Walkthrough

    Open every drawer, check reveals, and confirm the seat still fits before we close the job.
