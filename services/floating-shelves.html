Floating Shelves

Floating shelves look simple only when the hidden hardware, the studs, and the level line are right. We match wood or paint to the room and can add a warm LED channel under the front edge.

How we build it

  1. What the shelves must hold

    Books, objects, or display only. That sets thickness, rod depth, and how many studs we need.

  2. Map the wall

    Stud finder, level reference line, outlets, and spacing between shelves so objects fit.

  3. Choose the support

    Hidden rod brackets or a professional interlocking bracket. Masonry and metal studs need different fasteners.

  4. Concept finish

    Light wood, dark wood, or painted to match trim. Optional LED color temperature.

  5. Estimate

    Length, count, lighting, and wall type go into a written scope.

  6. Build the shelves

    Accurate rod holes or routed channels. Finish sanding and coating before they go on the wall.

  7. Install

    Fasten male brackets to studs, test-fit, lock with set screws, and hide the joint at the wall.

  8. Light and walkthrough

    If LED is included, we test the strip before the diffuser snaps in, then check load and level with you.
